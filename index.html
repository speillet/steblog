AI agents × Geospatial tools

GeoSkills

Helping AI agents work with geographic data.

On this IGN project, I worked on organising reusable skills and defining the use cases they should cover.

To turn a question about a place into a map, an agent needs to find a suitable source, then choose the right operations for the data. GeoSkills brings these geospatial capabilities into an agent’s workflow.
